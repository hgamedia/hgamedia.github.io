Process

Signing a customer

  1. Agree the advert type, contract length and extras with the calculator above. Screenshot the result for the customer.
  2. Get their artwork (logo, photos, offer, phone, Instagram/website), or their finished advert (see specs below).
  3. Email the office: business name, contact name, phone, email, NIF/CIF for the invoice, the quote screenshot, and the artwork.
  4. The office sends the invoice and the bank details. The advert goes live once the money arrives. Nobody takes cash.
  5. The customer gets a private QR link and a private page where they see their counted showings and scans.

Never promise numbers of showings, screens or bars. Every figure we give is counted, never guessed.

Walk-in (30 seconds)

  1. "Hi, are you the owner?" If not: leave a flyer, get the owner's name and WhatsApp.
  2. "We put adverts for local businesses on bar screens round here. People sit for hours in front of them: no skipping, no scrolling."
  3. "€20 a month for the whole network, locked for a year, and we design the advert free. Only 10 founding places."
  4. "Can I mock one up for you this week?" Get the WhatsApp number. That's the win for the day.

Rules

Who we take, what we never say

  • Never: other bars or venues that compete with the bars we're in.
  • Never: gambling or betting (house rule). The law says no: tobacco, vapes, e-cigarettes. Games, arcades and gaming shops are fine.
  • Best targets: transfers and taxis, currency exchange, hair and barbers, nails and beauty, day and boat trips, estate agents, tattoo, dentists and clinics, car hire, takeaways, gyms, English-run shops.
  • Towns: Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Fuengirola, La Cala, Marbella.
  • Never give screen counts, bar names or locations. Never invent a number.
  • You can design adverts for your customers and charge them for it yourself, or just send us their artwork.
